Camino: Vigo to Santiago

A birding and walking journal from Vigo to Santiago de Compostela along the Camino de Santiago in Galicia, Spain.

Along the Camino

In July 2026, I walked through the varied landscapes of Galicia, following the Camino from Vigo to Santiago de Compostela. Coastal scenery, farmland, villages and woodland provided many opportunities to observe birds along the way.

This chapter collects the birds I photographed and filmed during the journey. More species will be added as the journal continues to grow.
