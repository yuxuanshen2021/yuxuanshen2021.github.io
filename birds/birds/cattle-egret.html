Camino: Vigo to Santiago

Cattle Egret

Bubulcus ibis

牛背鹭

Location Along the Camino from Vigo to Santiago, Galicia, Spain
Date July 2026
Habitat Farmland and open grassland

Field Notes

I encountered this Cattle Egret in a field while walking the Camino de Santiago from Vigo to Santiago. Its compact white body and yellow bill stood out clearly against the dry vegetation.

Cattle Egrets are often found in farmland, pastures and grassland. They commonly follow grazing animals or agricultural activity, feeding on insects and other small animals disturbed from the ground.
