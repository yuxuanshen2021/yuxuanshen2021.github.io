Grey Heron

Ardea cinerea · 灰鹭

The Grey Heron is a large waterbird with long legs, a long neck, and a powerful pointed bill. It is often seen standing quietly beside the water while waiting for fish and other aquatic prey. This individual was photographed in Barcelona.

Date and Location 2026/07/22 Diagonal-Mar, Barcelona, Spain
Species Grey Heron
Scientific name Ardea cinerea
