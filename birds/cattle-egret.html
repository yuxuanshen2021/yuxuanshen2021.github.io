Cattle Egret

Bubulcus ibis

牛背鹭

Location Near a railway station in Barcelona, Spain
Date 18 July 2026
Habitat Farmland and open grassland

Field Notes

Cattle Egrets are commonly seen in farmland, pastures and grassland. They often follow livestock or agricultural activity, feeding on insects and other small animals disturbed from the ground.
