Monk Parakeet

Myiopsitta monachus · 和尚鹦鹉（灰胸鹦鹉）

The Monk Parakeet is a small, sociable parrot native to South America. It is easily recognized by its bright green plumage, grey face and breast, and noisy calls. Unlike most parrots, it builds large communal stick nests, often in palm trees. These individuals were photographed in Parc de la Ciutadella, Barcelona.
